About CarpCorral

Our Mission

CarpCorral is an FLL innovation project exploring how AI, sound, and automated sorting could help manage invasive carp while protecting native fish.

Our Technology

Our prototype uses a webcam, a Python AI model, an ESP32 microcontroller, a servo sorting gate, and a sound system.

This web application currently runs in demonstration mode. Its controls do not operate real hardware.
