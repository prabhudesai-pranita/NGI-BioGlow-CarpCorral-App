About CarpSort

Our Mission

CarpSort is a FIRST LEGO League BioGlow innovation project by the NextGen Innovators. We’re exploring how a camera, AI, and a sorting gate could help separate invasive carp from native fish in the Illinois River.

The Problem We’re Solving

Our project focuses on invasive silver and bighead carp. We asked: How could we identify these fish as they pass through a system and give native fish a route back to the river?

How Our Prototype Works

  1. Capture an image. A webcam sends images to a laptop running our Python program.
  2. Identify the fish. Our AI model predicts whether the image shows invasive carp, a native fish, or something it cannot confidently identify.
  3. Sort the fish. The laptop sends a command to an ESP32 microcontroller. The ESP32 moves the gate in our tabletop model to demonstrate separate paths for carp and native fish.
  4. Respond to carp. Our design uses carp detection as the trigger for sound. We’re exploring whether sound could help guide carp toward the sorting area.

What We Changed After Testing

We started by building a gate that could switch between two exits. Then we added a camera and trained the AI to recognize invasive carp and native fish. During testing, the model sometimes labeled native fish as invasive carp. That gave us a specific improvement to work on: retraining the model and checking how it handles uncertain images.

Our tabletop prototype demonstrates the sorting sequence. A river version would need further testing, including how the camera performs in cloudy water and how fish respond to the sound.
